Teaching

My teaching experience spans undergraduate and graduate courses in machine learning, control systems, and engineering.

CIS 5200 Machine Learning

University of Pennsylvania · Spring 2022 · Undergraduate
Teaching Assistant

This course covers the foundations of statistical machine learning, with an emphasis on probabilistic and statistical methods for prediction and clustering in high dimensions. Topics include regression, support vector machines, dimensionality reduction, expectation-maximization, hidden Markov models, and deep learning.

Teaching contributions: Designed homework and examination materials, taught classes, and held office hours.

ESE 5000 Linear Systems Theory

University of Pennsylvania · Fall 2021 · Graduate
Teaching Assistant

This graduate-level course focuses on continuous and discrete linear systems, including state-space representations, stability, controllability, observability, realization, and feedback stabilization.

Teaching contributions: Designed homework and examination materials, taught classes, and held office hours.

Programming Techniques

National Technical University of Athens · Spring 2017 · Undergraduate
Teaching Assistant

An undergraduate programming course covering fundamental programming concepts and hands-on programming through laboratory exercises.

Teaching contributions: Assisted students during laboratory sessions.
